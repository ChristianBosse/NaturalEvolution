# Code of Conduct

NEVO is meant to help people in hard times, whoever and wherever they are. The community building it should reflect that.

## Our commitment

We want participation in NEVO to be welcoming and free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and non-programmers.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Explain your reasoning.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good faith.
- Remember that people from many countries and languages take part.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Deliberately adding false or dangerous information to the project, especially medical or safety content
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, discussions, pull requests, and any other channels the project uses) and when someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban participants, depending on how serious and repeated the behavior is.
